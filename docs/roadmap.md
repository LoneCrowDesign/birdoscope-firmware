# Roadmap

Planned hardware and firmware work. For what exists today, see
[hardware variants](hardware_variants.md).

## Board Revisions

| Board                     | Status                         |
|---------------------------|--------------------------------|
| Analyze r0.1 (ESP32-S3)   | Current revision               |
| Analyze r0.4 (ESP32-S3)   | Releasing shortly              |
| Detect carrier (ESP32-C3) | Pinout planned, not fabricated |

The Analyze r0.1 covers the basic and plus tiers and has no second radio.
[Analyze r0.1 hardware](hardware/hardware_analyze_r01_esp32s3.md) documents its
pinout.

The Detect carrier pinout targets the ESP32-C3 Supermini, with tier
assignments in
[ESP32-C3 Supermini hardware](hardware/hardware_c3supermini.md). No board
config or build environment exists for it yet.

## Tier Consolidation

All of this is still in real-world testing, and the tiers may collapse into
three standard ones. Until then, you can build a detector from whatever hardware
you have on hand, at a flexible price point.
